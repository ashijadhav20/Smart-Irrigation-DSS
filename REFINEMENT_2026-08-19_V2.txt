SMART IRRIGATION DSS — REFINEMENT V2 (19 AUGUST 2026)

NEW / RETAINED FEATURES
1. Field geometry now offers BOTH:
   - Regular shape: Rectangle, Square, Circle, Triangle, Trapezoid with entered dimensions and orientation.
   - Irregular/actual boundary: draw polygon over satellite imagery or upload GeoJSON.
2. Every saved field is represented by a real WGS-84 polygon at its location.
3. Satellite view uses Esri World Imagery; OpenStreetMap remains an optional base layer.
4. Open-Meteo depth-wise soil moisture remains in the dashboard (0-1, 1-3, 3-9, 9-27, 27-81 cm).
5. Soil definition now has three routes:
   - Automatic ISRIC SoilGrids texture estimate (sand/silt/clay + USDA class).
   - Manual USDA textural-class selection.
   - User-entered sand/silt/clay percentages; dashboard automatically classifies USDA texture.
6. Soil panel displays FC and PWP defaults for the selected textural class.
7. Final 3D Terrain page added. It shows:
   - the saved field boundary on satellite imagery;
   - a 3D elevation surface around the same field;
   - the saved field boundary plotted in 3D;
   - min/max elevation and local relief.
8. 3D elevation uses Open-Meteo Elevation API. It is contextual terrain, not engineering-survey accuracy.
9. Sentinel-2 NDVI continues to use the saved field polygon.
10. Irrigation volume continues to use field plan area in m2.

ADDED: COMBINED ROOT-ZONE DEPLETION DECISION GRAPH
--------------------------------------------------
The Soil Moisture page now includes a presentation-ready decision graph combining:
- Current and forecast root-zone depletion Dr (mm)
- RAW irrigation threshold (mm)
- TAW total available water (mm)
- First forecast RAW-crossing point marked as IRRIGATE
- Dr/RAW percentage metric
- Clear message for irrigation required now, forecast trigger, or no trigger in horizon

The earlier depth-wise Open-Meteo soil-moisture trend, depletion/recharge-by-depth graph,
and root-zone soil-moisture forecast remain unchanged and available on the same page.
